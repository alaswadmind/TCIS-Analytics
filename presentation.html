Core Problem & Solution

The Carrier Challenge: Unstructured Feedback & Outage Latency

The Operational Challenge

Carriers handle massive volumes of incoming unstructured messages across digital channels. Manual human triaging introduces severe operational bottlenecks:

  • Delayed detection of regional fiber cuts and localized cell tower outages.
  • Inability to prioritize high-risk churn signals over routine FAQs.
  • Taxonomy fragmentation: customer service agents assigning conflicting root causes.
  • False alarms caused by subjective complaint assessment without statistical validation.

The TCIS Intelligence Engine

A high-throughput analytical pipeline converting unstructured text into structured, actionable operational decisions in under one second:

  • Complaint & Sentiment Parsing: Precise single-label scoring distinguishing actual grievances from inquiries.
  • Constrained Hierarchy (L1 ➔ L2 ➔ L3): Guaranteeing logical consistency between service domains and root causes.
  • Proactive Outage Surveillance: Statistical Poisson scanning alerting NOC teams before call center congestion occurs.
  • Generative Incident Summaries: Instant FLAN-T5 operational reports for engineering dispatch.

Mathematical Foundations

Poisson Surveillance, False Discovery Rate & Constrained Hierarchy

Temporal Surveillance • Poisson Process 24h Sliding Window

The 24h Poisson Outage Detection Engine

To determine whether a cluster spike represents a genuine incident or normal random variance, the system scans timestamps in a sliding 24-hour window ($W=24$) and computes the Poisson survival function:

$$P(X \ge k) = 1 - \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} \frac{\lambda^i e^{-\lambda}}{i!}$$ where $\lambda = \frac{N \cdot W}{\text{span}}$ is the baseline expected rate, and $k$ is the observed count.

Extreme low probabilities ($p < 0.001$) signal statistically significant surges that cannot be explained by random customer inquiry fluctuations.

Benjamini-Hochberg FDR Control (q-value)

Multiple Testing

Evaluating 228 clusters simultaneously drastically increases false positive risks. TCIS applies Benjamini-Hochberg False Discovery Rate (FDR) adjustments, computing adjusted $q\text{-values}$ to ensure only alerts with $q \le 0.01$ trigger operational escalation.

Constrained Hierarchical Decoding

Taxonomy Consistency

Raw neural classifiers can predict contradictory levels (e.g. Internet service with a SIM swap issue). TCIS applies a dynamic boolean mask $M_2$ and $M_3$ derived from carrier ground truth, restricting child argmax selections to legitimate taxonomy branches.

1. Why Macro-F1?

Extreme class imbalance in telecom logs means models can score 90%+ Accuracy simply by predicting dominant classes. Macro-F1 weights rare categories equally.

2. LinearSVC on Fine-Grained L3

LinearSVC scored 0.775 vs RoBERTa's 0.415 on specific issues, proving that regularized linear baselines excel when fine-grained categories have limited training samples.

3. RoBERTa Context Dominance

RoBERTa dominated semantic sentiment (0.931) and multi-label services (0.776) through its bidirectional masked attention mechanism.
